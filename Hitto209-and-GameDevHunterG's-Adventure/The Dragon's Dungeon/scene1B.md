# "I am Iarath Armash"
The crowd is filled with a stunned silence as you tell them your name.


One of the farmers is the first to break the silence.
> Iarath Armash? The famous adventurer?

You're shocked. You knew you were famous in the city, but you had no idea that your fame stretched out to the countryside. After registering this fact, you respond:
> Yes. I am Iarath Armash. Now, I was wondering if anyone could tell me why I feel like I just fell from the sky.

The crowd around you looks concerned when you say that last sentence. The woman who first asked for your name answers:
> The reason that you feel like you just fell from the sky, Iarath Armash, is because you did fall from the sky. Do you not remember?

You are shocked. Of course you don't remember. The last thing you remember is setting up camp in the mountains. But why were you in the mountains?
## Roll a DC (Difficulty Class) 10 history check

> [!TIP]
> You can use the [Dice Roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site to roll an ability check if you don't have your own dice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Iarath has a +3 modifier to history checks. Therefore, the outcome is the number rolled on the D20 plus an additional 3.
